This cocktail combines the sweetness of peach schnapps, the brightness of orange juice, and the tart finish of cranberry juice with smooth vodka. It’s fast, colorful, and ideal when you want something fun without a complicated recipe.

Notes

  • Fresh orange juice gives the drink a brighter and more natural flavor.
  • Pulp-free orange juice creates a smoother cocktail appearance.
  • For parties, multiply the recipe and mix it in a pitcher with plenty of ice.
  • You can substitute pineapple juice for orange juice for a tropical twist.

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker or mixing glass halfway with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka, peach schnapps, orange juice, and cranberry juice.
  3. Stir or shake gently until the mixture is chilled.
  4. Fill a serving glass with fresh ice and pour the cocktail over it.
  5. Garnish with orange slices and maraschino cherries before serving.
